# Role: warehouse_ops
# Topic: Lockout/Tagout (LOTO), Equipment Safety

Before performing maintenance, verify that all power is isolated. 
Don gloves, goggles, and steel-toe boots. 
Check for stored energy in hydraulic and pneumatic lines. 
Close the main disconnect and apply your lockout tag. 
Verify that all energy sources are labeled and locked. 
Alert your supervisor before testing. 
After work, remove locks and restore power safely.
