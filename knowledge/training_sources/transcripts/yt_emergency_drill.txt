# Role: warehouse_ops
# Topic: Emergency Response, Evacuation

Upon hearing the alarm, don PPE and move quickly to the exit. 
Check your zone for anyone left behind. 
Back off twenty meters from the structure and wait at the muster point. 
Alert the safety officer upon arrival. 
Do not re-enter the building until authorized. 
Verify attendance before resuming operations.
