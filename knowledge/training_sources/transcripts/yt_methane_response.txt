# Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0JXpOEFGkBg
# Role: warehouse_ops
# Topic: Methane Leak, Ventilation

When the methane alarm activates, verify your PPE immediately. 
Don the SCBA and check that your air supply valve is open. 
Check the surrounding zone for other personnel. 
Open both ventilation fans to high speed for two minutes. 
Back off ten meters after initiating ventilation. 
Alert the control room using the radio. 
Do not re-enter until gas readings are below threshold.
