# Role: warehouse_ops
# Topic: Electrical Safety, PPE

Check that electrical panels are properly labeled. 
Verify that the lockout procedure is engaged before touching conductors. 
Don insulated gloves and a face shield. 
Open the panel carefully and inspect for loose wiring. 
Alert electrical maintenance if arcing or burning odor is detected. 
Close and secure the panel after inspection.
