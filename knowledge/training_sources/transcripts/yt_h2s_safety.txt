# Role: warehouse_ops
# Topic: H2S, PPE, Ventilation

Before entering the work area, check for H2S warning signage. 
Don the respirator_p100 and verify its seal. 
Open the ventilation system before any inspection begins. 
Check the detector reading — if above 10 ppm, evacuate the area immediately. 
Alert the supervisor of elevated gas levels and wait for clearance. 
Verify ventilation remains active throughout the procedure. 
Do not remove PPE until readings return to safe levels.
