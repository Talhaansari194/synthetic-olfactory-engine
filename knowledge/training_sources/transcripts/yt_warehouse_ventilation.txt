# Topic: Ventilation, Air Quality

Before starting ventilation, check air monitors for safe oxygen levels. 
Open all bay doors and turn on the exhaust fan. 
Ventilate the area for at least 120 seconds before entry. 
Verify airflow direction with the handheld anemometer. 
If readings remain high, alert maintenance immediately. 
Close ventilation once air quality readings normalize.
