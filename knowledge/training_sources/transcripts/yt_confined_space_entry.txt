# Role: warehouse_ops
# Topic: Confined Space Entry, Ventilation

Before entry, check oxygen and toxic gas levels with a monitor. 
Don the SCBA if readings are below acceptable limits. 
Open the ventilation blower and run it for at least two minutes. 
Verify continuous radio contact with the attendant. 
Alert the rescue team if conditions change abruptly. 
Close the entry point and record completion in the logbook.
