# Role: warehouse_ops
# Topic: Fire Safety, Emergency Response

Check that fire extinguishers are inspected and pressurized. 
Verify that emergency exits are clear. 
Don flame-resistant gloves before handling flammable materials. 
Alert others immediately if smoke is detected. 
Open ventilation to remove fumes. 
Evacuate and call the fire team if flames persist.
