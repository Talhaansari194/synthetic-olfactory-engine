# Role: warehouse_ops
# Topic: Material Handling, Ergonomics

Before lifting materials, verify that the route is free of obstacles. 
Don gloves and safety boots. 
Check hoist straps and forklifts for defects. 
Open the ventilation fans to reduce dust. 
Alert maintenance if any malfunction occurs. 
Close and secure equipment after use.
