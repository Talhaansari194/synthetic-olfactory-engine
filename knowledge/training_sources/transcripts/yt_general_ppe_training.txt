# Topic: PPE, General Safety

Before beginning work, verify all PPE components are available. 
Check gloves, goggles, and respirator_p100 filters for defects. 
Don PPE following the sequence: gloves, respirator, goggles, vest. 
Verify that ventilation is operational before using solvents. 
Alert your supervisor if any PPE item is damaged or missing. 
Close all tools and isolate power after completing the shift.
